Why We Use n8n

n8n is the open-source workflow automation platform that gives you full control. Unlike closed SaaS tools, n8n runs on your infrastructure, keeps your data private, and never locks you in.

  • Self-hosted — runs on your servers, your cloud, your rules.
  • Open-source — audit the code, extend it, own it forever.
  • 400+ integrations — connects to virtually every SaaS tool and API.
  • Cost-effective — no per-execution fees that balloon as you scale.
